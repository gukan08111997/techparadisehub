Common questions




Do I need a technical background?
No. Excel, SQL and Python Basic start from zero. Check the "Who it is for" line on each course page to see where to begin.


What is the difference between a course and a crash course?
A full course covers a subject in depth over several weeks. A crash course covers the essentials in five days and suits people with limited time or a deadline.


Can I try a class before I enrol?
Yes. Book a free demo class from the contact page and we will confirm a slot by email.


Do I get a certificate?
You receive a course completion certificate after you submit your project.
